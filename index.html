    How the numbers work

    P_accept = clamp( (Rate/100) × fitFactor , 0.02 , 0.95 ) with fitFactor = 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0 / 1.3 / 1.6 for Fit 1–5.

    Expected Yield ranks by EIM = P_accept × IF / (weeks ÷ 4.345) — impact actually captured per month. Balanced ranks by a weighted, min-max-normalized blend of prestige, acceptance odds, speed and cost.

    Everything is repeatable: same inputs → same ranking. The only judgement is the Fit score, and the rubric pins that down. Nothing is stored — reloading clears the desk.

    Evidence

    Fit-first ranking follows Rees et al., Perspect Med Educ 2022 (n=691): prioritising fit doubled the odds of first-choice acceptance (OR 2.11), while prioritising impact reduced them (OR 0.37). Timeline defaults come from the same survey. The Expected Yield formula is a simplification of the Markov decision model in Salinas & Munch, PLOS ONE 2015.
